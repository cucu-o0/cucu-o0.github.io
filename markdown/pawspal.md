## The App

**PawsPal** matches families with the right shelter pet — by lifestyle fit, not by browsing endless listings.

You answer a few questions about your household — context, kids, other pets, previous experience — and **PawsPal** ranks the pets that actually suit you and your family.

- **Fit before photos.** Pets are ranked by how well they suit your daily life.
- **Save and send.** Shortlist your favourites and pass them to the shelter to start the adoption.
- **Fewer returns.** Better matches up front mean fewer pets coming back to the shelter.

## The Data

*All pets shown are real animals waiting for a home in Barcelona, via [@adopta.loveanimals.bcn](https://www.instagram.com/adopta.loveanimals.bcn/).*

Adoption listings are usually thin and inconsistent — a photo, a name, a couple of lines. Not enough to match anyone reliably.

> The goal is **precise, complete pet data** — age, size, health, behaviour with kids and other animals, energy level, experience needed, legal restrictions. AI fills the gaps and normalises what shelters publish, so every pet is described on the same terms.

## Design requirements

| | |
|---|---|
| 🙂 **Easy to use** | Anyone can find a pet in a few taps. No manuals, no jargon. |
| 🛡️ **Reliable** | Tested, and honest about what it knows — no invented pet facts. |
| 💸 **Free, or nearly free** | Runs at almost zero cost, so a shelter could actually afford it. |
| ⚡ **Fast and flexible** | Quick to load, and easy to extend to new shelters and new data. |


## Future Improvements

💡 Deeper match reasoning — not just *what* fits, but *why* — so the decision feels informed, not impulsive. Held back for now to keep running costs low.
